## Fun projects

* [**Credere**](https://github.com/yashraisaini/credere): Splitwise felt boring and too US-focused for our reading week trip through Europe, so I built my own. It does live currency conversion, scans receipts and splits them across the group, and even adds card fees based on each person's bank plan. My friends and I used it the whole trip.
* [**Contrail**](https://github.com/yashraisaini/contrail): I fly a lot and love Flighty, so I made my own version. Live flight data, a passport that maps everywhere you've flown, and a 3D virtual fridge where you collect magnets from places you've been.
